Process Synchronization
-----------------------

- the coordination and control of concurrent processes to ensure correct and predictable outcomes.

purpose
-------
- to prevent race conditions, data inconsistencies, and resource conflicts that may arise when multiple processes access shared resources simultaneously.

-Synchronization makes sure only one process updates shared data at a time.


Why we need it ?
---------------

When several processes or threads share data (a variable, a file, a buffer), the final result can depend on the order in which they run. That is a race condition.

Example: counter = 5. Process A does counter++ and process B does counter--. Each is really three machine steps:

load  R, counter
add/sub R, 1
store counter, R

If A and B interleave (A loads 5, B loads 5, A stores 6, B stores 4), the final value is 4 or 6 instead of the correct 5. Synchronization makes sure only one process manipulates shared data at a time.


We have four types of solutions to the process synchronization problem −

  1. Locks
  2. Semaphores
  3. Monitors
  4. Interrupt Disable
